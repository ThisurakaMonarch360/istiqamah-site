Privacy Policy

Istiqamah is a free prayer companion for Muslims in Sri Lanka, made by an independent developer. This policy describes what information the app handles, where it is kept, and who can see it.

18 November 2026

2Data stored on the device

The app keeps the following on your phone, in its private app storage:

  • Prayer log: prayers you mark as prayed, make-up (qada) prayers and rest days.
  • Favourites: duas you save.
  • Settings: zone or district, language, theme and alert preferences.

This data is not accessible to the developer. It is kept until you delete it.

2.1 Deleting your data

Open Settings → Apps → Istiqamah → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything listed above.

2.2 Backups

The app's backup feature creates a file containing the data above and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only to the destination you choose. If Android's device backup to your Google account is enabled, Android may include the app's data in that backup, under Google's Privacy Policy.

4Third-party services

4.1 everyayah.com

When you play a recitation, the app requests the audio from everyayah.com over HTTPS. As with any web request, everyayah.com receives your IP address and the recitation requested. Audio is streamed and not saved on the device. No other information is sent.

4.2 Google Play

Google provides installation, updates and crash reporting for all apps under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer may see aggregated, anonymous statistics from Google Play, such as install counts and crash reports, which do not identify you.

4.3 Your phone's text-to-speech

If you tap Read aloud, the app passes one sentence, the next prayer and its time, to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's). Nothing else is passed. The engine is part of your phone, not of this app, and follows its own privacy policy.

4.4 External links

Some items, such as this policy, the Play Store page, credits and the feedback email, open in your browser or email app. The app sends no data with them. Those apps and websites follow their own privacy policies.

5Security

Data is held in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. The developer keeps no copy on any server. Recitation is streamed over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

6Your rights

The developer collects no personal data, so holds nothing about you to access, correct or delete. You control all data the app stores and can delete it at any time (section 2.1).

For questions or concerns under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, or any other privacy law, contact the developer (section 9).

7Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from any user, including children.

8Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example by adding a feature that uses the network, this page will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
